LEGAL & TRUST COMPLIANCE

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 2026 | Last Updated: September 6, 2026

1. Introduction & Commitment

GCertainity AI Engineering ("GCertainity", "we", "our", or "us") operates the website gcertainity.com, candidate fellowship applications, and institutional university accelerator portals. We are committed to maintaining the highest standards of data privacy, confidentiality, and technical security for candidates, learners, university leadership, and enterprise partners.

Zero Data Monetization Guarantee

GCertainity maintains a strict zero-data-monetization policy. We do NOT sell, rent, trade, or monetize applicant, learner, or university institutional data to third-party ad networks or data brokers under any circumstances.

2. Information We Collect

We collect information directly provided by users during inquiry submissions, fellowship applications, and institutional briefing requests:

  • Personal Identifiable Information (PII): Full name, official email address, phone/WhatsApp number, current academic stage, institution/university name, and professional designation.
  • Telemetry & Build Performance: Code commit hashes, container deployment metrics, evaluation scores (RAGAS benchmarks), and public verification links hosted on gcertainity.com/verify/....
  • System & Usage Logs: IP address, browser type, device metadata, and access timestamps collected automatically for infrastructure stability and security auditing.

3. How We Use Your Information

The information collected is used exclusively for the following operational purposes:

  • Operational 1-Hour Revert Guarantee: To contact applicants and institutional leaders within our binding 1-hour inquiry response window.
  • Fellowship Instruction & Verification: To deliver 1-Year Live Commercial AI Engineering training, facilitate RAG/multi-agent build sprints, and issue co-badged verified credentials.
  • Academic Credit Mapping: To align capstone project outcomes with university academic credit frameworks (UGC/AICTE) and mandatory internship requirements.
  • Hiring Partner Connections: To connect verified AI FDE candidates with enterprise hiring partners with explicit candidate consent.

4. Data Security & Encryption Standards

We deploy multi-layered technical safeguards to protect all stored and transmitted data. All network communication with gcertainity.com is encrypted in transit using TLS 1.3 protocol. Stored databases and backup records are encrypted at rest using AES-256 standards.

5. Candidate & Institutional Data Rights

Under applicable data protection laws, candidates and institutional partners have the right to request access, correction, export, or complete deletion of their personal information. To exercise any data rights, please contact our compliance officer at admissions@gcertainity.com or partnerships@gcertainity.com.
